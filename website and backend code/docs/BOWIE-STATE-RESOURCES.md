## Which service should I choose?

- Counseling Services: routine counseling appointments; the official page says counseling is free for enrolled BSU students. The main page currently lists Robinson Hall, Suite 2800 and Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5:30 p.m. Some older university pages show different details, so confirm when booking.
- Urgent/after-hours counseling: the main counseling page lists 301-860-4164 and ProtoCall support. This differs from the routine appointment number.
- Freshman/peer support: ask about adjusting to college and peer programs. Peer guidance has a different role from professional counseling.
- Wellness Center: routine student primary care. Call about services, availability and fees.
- Disability Support Services: ask about accommodations, required documentation and the request process.
- LGBTQIA Resource Center: supportive campus community, programs and resources.
- Academic Advisement: course planning and academic decisions.
- Financial wellness: the university wellness page directs students to financial aid for individual financial literacy guidance.
- Public Safety: campus safety assistance, separate from counseling.

## Telecounseling

Bowie's counseling page says students must be physically in Maryland for telecounseling. It describes a one-time consultation/referral option for students outside Maryland. Contact Counseling Services to confirm the option that applies to you.

## Immediate help

For immediate danger in the U.S., call 911. For crisis support, call/text 988 or visit https://988lifeline.org. Campus Calm does not contact emergency services, monitor conversations for emergencies or assess whether someone is safe.
